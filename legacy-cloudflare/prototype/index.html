Roles and responsibilities

Tenant: report an issue, track progress and acknowledge completed work.

Facility manager: review requests, allocate work, monitor progress and approve or return completed jobs.

Contractor or internal staff: record progress, before and after photos, work time and overtime, then sign off.

Portfolio manager: oversee the workload across facilities and outstanding jobs.

For a live rollout

Real accounts and server-enforced permissions, shared database and secure file storage, notifications, audit history, backup and retention policies, and a tested deployment are required. Agree approval rules, overtime authorisation and what a tenant signature means before rollout.
