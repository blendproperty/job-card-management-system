# Blend Job Cards implementation plan

1. **Set the first operational release.** A tenant signs in, raises a job for their space and follows its progress. A qualifying job goes to the right trade automatically. Exceptions go to a property, asset or facilities manager. The trade accepts the job, records a visit, captures before and after photos and obtains the tenant's acknowledgement. The manager checks the evidence and closes the job. The Blend Field analysis informs the office, field, evidence and notification design. The Planado analysis informs scheduling, repeat work and follow-up. The current brief determines the scope and manager-led onboarding.

2. **Model the portfolio and contacts.** Use organisations, property owners, buildings and units. Link people to properties or units with effective dates and a role scope. PMs, AMs and FMs enter or upload First name, Surname, Company, Email and Contact number for both registers. A tenant can have several spaces. For trades, add company, person, trades, building coverage, availability, qualifications and expiry dates. Normalise emails and phone numbers, flag collisions and record the importing manager. Invite a verified email address from its directory record and link the account to the approved units or trade scope. End access promptly when a tenant moves or a contract expires. The registers currently capture details and site associations. Invitations and dispatch eligibility are separate implementation work.

3. **Build the three role views.** Tenant dashboard shows authorised spaces, job references, appointments, progress and sign-off requests. New Job uses the signed-in tenant's contact details and defaults to their sole authorised space. For multiple spaces, it offers a picker. A manager can log on behalf of an authorised tenant and the system records both identities. The Trade portal lists only assigned jobs, with accept or decline, scheduling, arrival, time, evidence and sign-off. The PM, AM and FM workspace shows building-scoped queues for new, unassigned, overdue, awaiting sign-off and ready to close jobs, with assignment history and notification outcomes. Scope administration explicitly by role and property before inviting production users.

4. **Capture and route job intake.** Store a job reference, organisation, building/unit, requester, contact snapshot, trade, issue, access window, priority and requirements. Model status history separately. Apply an approved, versioned dispatch rule keyed by building and trade. Filter workers by active coverage, required qualifications, availability and site access. Use the configured priority and workload tie-break to pick a single eligible worker. Create the assignment and notification event in the same transaction and log the matched rule version. Route missing or ambiguous matches, emergency exceptions, declines, expired acknowledgements and unavailable trades to a manager queue with the reason. Record manual assignment and reassignment with actor and reason. Dispatch rules should never expand the worker's general access to the building. The current app offers trade matching after manager approval. Building-specific rules and direct dispatch are planned.

5. **Run the visit and capture evidence.** Model assignments, visits, time entries and media separately from the job. Record acceptance, scheduled window, arrival, start, pause, resume and finish. Derive elapsed and on-site times and send overtime for approval when required. Require before and after photos per the job rule. Keep photos in private storage and save phase, uploader, server time and device capture time. Offline changes and uploads use durable queues and idempotency keys. Failed uploads stay visible until synced. Record no access or follow-up as an explicit outcome with a reason, and allow rescheduling without overwriting history.

6. **Obtain tenant acknowledgement and close.** Bind the signer, sign-off wording and version, acknowledgement time and evidence references to the completed visit. Offer an authenticated portal link when the tenant cannot sign in person. Record declined or unavailable sign-off with a reason and a manager review. Preserve the original sign-off, and create a new event if work changes after signature. Show closure to the tenant once the manager accepts the evidence and resolves exceptions. Blend should review when ordinary job acknowledgement is sufficient and whether any specific document requires an advanced electronic signature under sections 13 and 37–38 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. [ECT Act](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a25-02.pdf)

7. **Deliver and track notifications.** Emit in-app alerts on receipt, assignment, reassignment, appointment changes, sign-off requests and closure. Send WhatsApp assignment and reminders via the dedicated Job Cards WhatChimp sender once registered, with email fallback when WhatsApp is unavailable or fails. Store recipient, template, channel, deduplication key, provider reference, attempt count and delivery outcome in a transactional outbox and delivery log. Verify and deduplicate callbacks. Link to a scoped job page using an expiring token where applicable and keep addresses and evidence out of messages. Escalate exhausted delivery attempts. Test real sends only with approved recipients. WhatsApp remains dependent on Meta verification and templates for +27 60 017 8025.

8. **Enforce privacy and security.** Apply Postgres row-level security and private media access for organisations, property scopes, tenant accounts and assigned workers. Separate tenant-visible notes from internal records. Audit role grants, import, dispatch overrides, time edits, sign-off and closure. Use privileged server credentials only for designated backend operations. Document the lawful basis, privacy notice, retention, information requests, access revocation and processor agreements covering messaging and storage. Review the handling of contact data, signatures and photos with Blend before onboarding. [POPIA](https://www.gov.za/documents/protection-personal-information-act)

9. **Ship and test on the dedicated VPS.** Build Next.js and the Trade web app from a reviewed GitHub revision in Docker. Apply Postgres migrations against the dedicated Job Cards Supabase stack with a tested backup and rollback path. Traefik serves the app and API over TLS. Keep production keys and provider configuration outside Git. Validate tenant intake and multi-unit access, valid and malformed imports, automatic and manual assignment, declines and reassignment, notification fallback and callback replay, handset photo capture, offline restart and sync, times and overtime, sign-off, reopening, closure and tenant progress. Verify isolation across tenants, trades and organisations, expired links, private media, backup restore and HTTPS. Have actual tenant, trade and manager users walk through a job before operational rollout. Recurring maintenance, expanded reporting and finance integration can follow the core workflow.
